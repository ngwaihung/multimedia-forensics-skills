# Image manipulation examination

**Question:** Was a specified image or region materially altered after capture? Define the authentication criteria and whether routine development, cropping, and platform recompression count as alteration. Keep object/person identification and event reconstruction as separate content-analysis questions. Follow [SWGDE 18-I-001-2.0, §§ 6-9](https://www.swgde.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-03-03-Best-Practices-for-Image-Authentication-18-I-001-2.0.pdf) for the structure-plus-content examination and review requirements.

1. Review the request, limit task-irrelevant context, and state competing propositions. Assess whether image quality, quantity, and reference material support the proposed examination. Seek a camera original, earlier copy, related image series, or video if they could change the opinion. Document any decision to limit or decline an examination.
2. Preserve and identify the supplied item per [evidence integrity](../references/evidence-integrity.md). Analyze verified copies, map orientation and original pixel coordinates, and record all steps contemporaneously.
3. Examine **structure**: byte-level format/packaging, metadata, compression, dimensions, previews, processing history, and applicable sensor traces per [metadata analysis](../references/metadata-and-container-analysis.md). Record what this file generation permits you to test.
4. Examine **content**: the claimed region, artifacts, physical scene consistency, and photographic conditions at native scale. State hypotheses such as compositing, local retouching, staging, in-camera optical effect, or ordinary re-encoding.
5. Select discriminating tests from [image authentication](../references/image-authentication.md). Test suspicious and comparable control regions, preserving parameters and outputs. Avoid drawing a conclusion from an error-level image or a single compression map.
6. Try to falsify the preferred interpretation with comparison exports, camera exemplars, or benign processing simulations when warranted. Evaluate structure and content together and document contrary evidence.
7. Report exact coordinates, observed features, alternatives, sensitivity limits, and a qualified opinion under the stated criteria. Submit final findings for independent review by a comparably trained examiner; record the outcome. Until then, mark the opinion preliminary and unreviewed.
