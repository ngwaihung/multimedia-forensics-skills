# Video continuity examination

**Question:** Is the encoded sequence continuous over a specified interval? Define whether the concern is a visible cut, missing captured time, reordering, an undisclosed edit, or source provenance. A file-level examination does not establish that depicted events actually occurred. Follow [SWGDE 23-V-001-1.2 (7 March 2024), §§ 4-8](https://www.swgde.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/23-V-001-1.2-Best-Practices-for-Digital-Video-Authentication.pdf).

1. Clarify the proposition, alleged source device, recording date, acquisition chain, and disputed interval. Limit task-irrelevant context. Assess whether the requested continuity examination is suitable and seek native files or matched exemplars when available.
2. Preserve the container per [evidence integrity](../references/evidence-integrity.md); identify whether it is camera-native, proprietary, exported, or a platform derivative. Inventory tracks, peripheral data, timebases, edit lists, frame-rate behavior, and duration. Document proprietary validation features and any export losses.
3. Use validated tools to examine global format, binary structure, and attributes; cross-check material metadata interpretations. Create a packet/frame timeline with PTS, DTS, duration, keyframes, decode errors, and sample/track offsets. Reconcile presentation and decode order, and map candidate events to source timestamps and indices.
4. Conduct systematic global and local visual inspection. Review neighboring frames and synchronized audio. Test scene motion, lighting, physical continuity, noise, encoding, sound bed, and lip synchronization; account for normal cuts, variable frame rate, B-frames, dropped frames, and in-camera pause. Route claims about the audio stream to [audio authentication](../../audio-authentication/SKILL.md).
5. Form and test rival explanations for each candidate event. Compare an earlier file, acquisition log, or matched device/export exemplar if available. Distinguish observations from measurements and state timing or similarity uncertainty. If only visible content changes, do not assert that media time was deleted without independent support.
6. Preserve extraction commands, stream/frame hashes or equivalent checks where feasible, and representative neighboring frames. Report supporting and contrary observations and whether results are consistent with an original, inconsistent with an original, or inconclusive under the specified proposition. Submit final findings for independent review by a comparably trained examiner; until then mark them preliminary.
